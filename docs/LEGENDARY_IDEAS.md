## REPUBLIC FACTION VIBE CHECK

### ✅ **What's Working:**
- **Thematic Coherence**: Bureaucracy, authorization, committees - flavor is excellent
- **Defensive Identity**: Guard spam, high HP, Shield - clearly the defensive faction
- **Requisition Mechanic**: Unique, thematic, creates interesting decision-making
- **Faction Contrast**: Completely different from Megacorp (swarm) and Confederate (synergy)

### ⚠️ **Concerns:**

   - Req 7 on Senator Okoye requires banking 2+ energy per turn for multiple turns
   - Req 8 (if used on Admiral Chen) is turn 8+ assuming perfect ramp
   - **Suggestion:** Test extensively, may need to lower thresholds by 1-2
 - Nil issue. As of turn 5, you start with 8 energy. Careful playing allows a player to get Req bonuses BEFORE playing their cards. You can take actions before spending energy if clever about it

   - Nil issue. The Republic should FEEL reactive
