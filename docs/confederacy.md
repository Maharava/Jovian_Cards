# The Outer Confederacy

## Overview
**The Outer Confederacy** is a loose alliance of independent settlements, moons, and stations beyond the Asteroid Belt. Born from necessity and forged in the vacuum of the Outer System, they represent humanity's frontier spirit: rugged, independent, and unwilling to bow to the distant bureaucracy of Earth.

They are the newest "faction" on the galactic stage. To the Republic, they are a legal headache; to the Megacorps, they are a market to be exploited.

*   **Archetype:** Guerilla / Combo / Synergy / Midrange
*   **Motto:** "Free Sky, Free Void."
*   **Visual Style:** A mix of **Space Western / Industrial** (worn metal, repurposed mining equipment, kinetic weapons) and **Bio-Punk** (clean white medical aesthetics, bioluminescent blues, organic curves) from their Jovian allies.

---

## Origins & Structure
The Confederacy began as a trade union of mining colonies on the moons of Jupiter and Saturn. The "Europa Incident"—when the Republic demanded the surrender of the "mutant" Jovian Sisters and the Outer Worlds refused—solidified their alliance and de-facto independence.

The Confederacy is a decentralized democracy run by a chaotic but fast-moving **Council of Captains**. They have no standing army, instead relying on a civilian militia where every ship is a warship and every miner a soldier.

## The Jovian Sisters
The Confederacy provides asylum to the Jovian Sisters, the genetically engineered children of the scientist Jupiter. In exchange, the Sisters provide advanced technology and act as powerful "hero" units for the Confederate cause. While many operate independently, they are the heart of the Confederacy's military and technological strength.

## Strategic Doctrine
The Confederacy cannot win a straight fight against the Republic Navy or a full Megacorp fleet. They rely on **Guerilla Warfare** and **Unit Quality**.
*   **Hit and Run:** Strike fast, do damage, and fade into the asteroid fields.
*   **Synergy:** Combined arms tactics where individually strong units become significantly more powerful when working together.
*   **Sustain:** High access to Healing and Guard allows key units to survive multiple turns and accrue value.
