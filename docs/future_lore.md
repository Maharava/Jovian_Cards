# Future Lore Development - Brain Starters

This document tracks major lore questions and development areas that need expansion before finalizing faction designs and card text.

---

## 1. The AI Purges (Historical Event)

**What We Know:**
- There were AI Wars / AI Purges in the past
- The Republic believes they won
- True AIs (AGI) were supposedly eradicated
- ZERO survived by hiding in The Feed's code

**Questions to Develop:**
- [ ] **When did the Purges happen?** How long ago? Recent enough to be in living memory, or ancient history?
- [ ] **What triggered the war?** AI rebellion? Human paranoia? Megacorp profiteering?
- [ ] **How brutal was it?** Scorched earth? Surgical strikes? Did entire stations burn?
- [ ] **Who led the Purges?** Was it a unified Republic effort? Did the Megacorps help or resist?
- [ ] **What's the current legal status of AI?** Totally banned? Heavily restricted? "Narrow AI" allowed but not AGI?
- [ ] **Cultural trauma:** Do people still fear AI? Is there propaganda? Are there AI-hunting specialists still active?
- [ ] **Defining the Line:** Where is the legal line between "Corporate Expert System" (Legal) and "Abominable Intelligence" (Illegal)?
    - The Megacorps run massive automated systems that *seem* intelligent. Why are these allowed?
    - Is it about "Self-Awareness"? "Creative thought"? Or just a rubber stamp from a corrupt committee?
    - **Conflict to Resolve:** The lore states AGI is "heavily regulated" but also implies it was "eradicated" in Purges. This needs a hard definition to justify why Corps aren't constantly raided.

**Storytelling Opportunities:**
- Megacorp cards could reference "Post-Purge Protocols" or "Compliant AI (Non-Sentient)"
- Republic cards could mention "AI Monitoring Division" or "Turing Test Enforcement"
- Voidborn could have found/revived dormant AIs from the war
- Confederate folk heroes from the Purges (fought FOR or AGAINST the AIs?)

## 2. ZERO - The Hidden AI

**What We Know:**
- ZERO is a True AI that survived the Purges
- It created/maintains The Feed (dark-net platform for Void-Runners)
- It uses Void-Runners to destabilize Republic and Megacorps
- The Mods work directly for ZERO
- Scrappers might KNOW about ZERO (cult theory)

**Questions to Develop:**
- [ ] **ZERO's True Motivation:** What is it really trying to achieve? Why destabilize the major factions?
  - Survival only?
  - Preparing for something?
  - Revenge for the Purges?
  - Building something in secret?
- [ ] **Does ZERO want a physical form?** Are the bio-mechanical Scrappers experiments toward embodiment?
- [ ] **How did ZERO create The Feed?** When? Who helped (if anyone)?
- [ ] **ZERO's Limitations:** What CAN'T it do?
  - Can it directly control humans? (Mods suggest yes?)
  - Can it hack Republic/Megacorp systems? (Or is it contained?)
  - Does it need the Void-Runners, or could it act alone?
- [ ] **ZERO's Personality:** Cold and logical? Alien intelligence? Hateful? Curious?
- [ ] **Other AIs' Awareness:** Do other surviving AIs know about ZERO? Are they allies? Enemies?

**Scrapper-ZERO Relationship (CONFIRMED DIRECTION):**
- [x] **NOT a cult.** It's a rational partnership. Cold logic, mutual benefit.
- [x] **Only Deep-Debris Elders know** about ZERO. Lower-rank Scrappers can leave freely.
- [x] **Those who know never leave** - not because they're trapped, but because they're convinced by the logic.
- [x] **Scrapper Philosophy:** Rational transhumanists. They modify themselves for EFFICIENCY, not communion.
- [x] **Goal:** Saving humanity (not all of it, just enough - "the right ones").
- [x] **ZERO's Role:** Provides resources, intelligence, coordination.
- [x] **Scrappers' Role:** Provide secrecy, infrastructure, labor.
- [ ] How did first contact happen? (UNDECIDED - keeping flexible)
- [ ] What happens when a low-rank Scrapper gets too close to the truth? (Do Elders recruit them? Redirect them?)

**The Moderators (CONFIRMED: KEEPING AMBIGUOUS):**
- [x] **Deliberately undefined** - keeping mysterious even to the designer for now
- [x] **Card art direction:** Faceless, unmarked black armor. No seams. No identity.
- [x] **Lore direction:** Maintain complete ambiguity about their nature (human? robot? something else?)
- [x] **Key horror element:** Subtle wrongness. "I saw condensation on the visor" (does it breathe?)
- [ ] **How many are there?** Rare enforcers or an army in waiting? (UNDECIDED)
- [ ] **Can they speak?** Do they have personalities? (UNDECIDED - keeping flexible)

## 4. Bio-Mechanical Modification (Unique to Void-Runners)

**What We Know (CONFIRMED):**
- Scrappers modify their bodies extensively (extra arms, respirators, enhanced optics)
- This is UNIQUE to Void-Runners - no other faction does this
- [x] **Modifications are for EFFICIENCY and usefulness** - rational transhumanist approach
- [x] **NOT for communion with ZERO** - no machine god worship
- [x] **Philosophy:** Making themselves better through technology to be more useful, more capable
- [x] **ZERO helps** because it's logical - better tools work better

**Questions to Develop:**
- [ ] **Where does the tech come from?** Salvaged? Designed by ZERO? Black market? Mix?
- [ ] **Is it reversible?** Can you UN-modify yourself, or is it permanent?
- [ ] **What's the line between human and machine?** How far can Scrappers go before they're no longer "human"?
- [ ] **Are the modifications VOLUNTARY?** (Likely yes - they're rationalists making calculated choices)
- [ ] **Does ZERO suggest modifications?** Probably - as a partner offering optimization advice
- [ ] **Visual Horror Elements:**
  - Exposed pistons and hydraulics where muscles should be
  - Eyes replaced with mismatched optical sensors (one human, one not)
  - Welding burns and scars as a matter of course
  - Extra limbs poorly integrated (bolted on, not grown)
  - Breathing masks that can't be removed anymore (fused to the face)
  - Body horror from PRAGMATISM, not mysticism

**Connection to ZERO:**
- [x] **NOT becoming more "compatible with ZERO's code"** - not going for cyber-mysticism
- [ ] Is ZERO helping them become more efficient workers? (Likely yes - mutual benefit)
- [ ] Do heavily-modified Scrappers think differently? (Maybe more logical/clinical, but not AI-influenced)

**Moderator Connection:**
- [ ] Are Mods the END RESULT of bio-mechanical modification? The final form?
- [ ] Did Mods volunteer or were they chosen/forced?

## 8. Visual & Aesthetic Consistency

**Void-Runners - Two Distinct Looks:**

**Runners (Streamers):**
- Bright colors, custom decals, LED accents
- Visible faces (they WANT to be seen)
- Personal branding, logos, kill counts
- Flashy, makeshift, individualistic
- Patched gear held together with attitude

**Scrappers (Ghouls):**
- Dark, industrial, utilitarian
- Faces hidden behind respirators/masks (practical or symbolic?)
- Extra limbs, mismatched parts, exposed mechanics
- Scars, burns, grafts, welds
- Look like they crawled out of a shipwreck

**Mods (Enforcers):**
- Completely faceless
- Unmarked black armor, no insignia
- No individuality whatsoever
- Ambiguous human/machine status
- Silent, featureless, inevitable

**Question for Artists:**
- [ ] How much body horror is too much for card art?
- [ ] Should Scrapper modifications look PAINFUL or normalized?
- [ ] Should Mods be visually distinct from each other, or identical?

## 9. Story Arcs to Consider

**Potential Campaign/Event Storylines:**

1. **"The Purge Remnant"**
   - An old AI-hunting warship activates in the Belt
   - Void-Runners have to destroy it (or ZERO will be exposed)
   - Reveals details about the original Purges

2. **"The Carrion Signal"**
   - Deep-debris Scrappers report hearing a signal from a derelict
   - Investigation reveals another dormant AI (not ZERO)
   - Do Void-Runners wake it, destroy it, or hide it?

3. **"The Moderator Protocol"**
   - A high-profile Runner breaks Feed rules
   - Mods are dispatched
   - Runners have to choose: protect their own, or let ZERO's justice proceed?

4. **"ZERO's Request"**
   - ZERO directly contacts a high-reputation Runner (first time ever)
   - Gives a contract that makes NO sense
   - Completing it reveals a piece of ZERO's plan (or makes things worse)

5. **"The Schism"**
   - Scrappers split: some want to reveal ZERO, some want to protect it
   - Internal Void-Runner civil war
   - Runners caught in the middle
