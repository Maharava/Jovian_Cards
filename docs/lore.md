# World of Jovian Cards: A Setting Guide (V2)

## The Solar System
The game takes place entirely within our Solar System. Humanity has colonized the inner planets, the Asteroid Belt, and the moons of Jupiter and Saturn. There is **no Faster-Than-Light (FTL)** travel; travel between planets takes weeks or months, relying on efficient fusion drives and orbital mechanics.

---

## The Origin Lore: Jupiter's Legacy

**Jupiter (The Mother)** was a brilliant geneticist who unlocked the key to human psychic potential. Banned by the Republic, she went underground.
*   **The Virus (30 Years Ago):** She detonated a viral bomb on **Ceres Station** that didn't kill, but rewrote DNA. Within thirty years, **Psychics** (humans with minor active genes) made up about 0.2% of the human population.
*   **The Children (The Family):** Years later, Jupiter used refined versions of this research to genetically engineer a specific family of post-humans: the **Jovian Sisters**. They are NOT random mutations; they are vat-grown, perfected beings. There are 16 known siblings; Europa, the eldest, is now 25.
*   **The Conflict:** The refusal of the massive Europa City-States to hand over the eldest sister (then 13 years old) triggered the formation of the Confederacy (now 12 years old).

## Cosmic Mysteries & Metaphysics

*   **Psychics:** Humans (descendants of the Ceres incident) with the active gene. They can manipulate forces like gravity, read emotions, or project energy.
*   **The Jovian Sisters:** A unique transhuman family. They possess far more stable and potent physiology than standard psychics.
*   **Psy-deaf:** A rare anomaly, people who are completely immune to psychic intrusion and resistant to telekinetic force.
*   **"That-between-gravity":** The Voidborn. Powerful psychics like **Europa** and **Metis** hear whispers from the void. It implies that Jupiter's work may have breached a barrier to something... else.

## Major Powers & Factions

### 1. The United Republic of Sol ("The Republic")
The colossal, ancient government of humanity, comprising 22 billion citizens across Earth, Mars, Luna, Venus, and countless stations.
*   **Structure:** "The Mother and Her Three Daughters."
    *   **Earth (The Mother):** Ancient, crowded, gridlocked.
    *   **Mars (First Daughter):** Industrial powerhouse, fully terraformed.
    *   **Luna (Second Daughter):** Research and transit hub.
    *   **Venus (Third Daughter):** Floating orbital luxury cities.
*   **Nature:** A sleeping giant paralyzed by its own fear of tyranny. Governed by endless committees and suffocating red tape.
*   **Power:** Technically unstoppable. It controls 90% of humanity's resources, but its decision-making is so slow (decades-long) that other factions operate in its blind spots.
*   **Stance:** It views the Confederacy as a legal headache and the Jovians as unclassified assets.

### 2. The Outer Confederacy
A volatile alliance of over a hundred independent settlements, moons, and stations beyond the Asteroid Belt.
*   **Nature:** A "political miracle" holding together a massive disparity of cultures. Born from the "Europa Incident," they were granted advanced technology by the mysterious **Jupiter** in exchange for sheltering her children.
*   **Structure:** It is a hodge-podge of conflicting beliefs and designs.
    *   **Europa:** Three massive cities hold 35% of the total population.
    *   **Callisto:** One city accounts for half the crime of the entire union.
    *   **Titan:** A dozen industrial settlements produce half the steel used by the other sixty.
*   **Role:** They provide asylum to the **Jovian Sisters**. In exchange, they use Jupiter's technology to resist corporate predation.

### 3. The Megacorps
Technically Republic-chartered entities, the Megacorps have amassed independent power due to the Republic's bureaucratic inertia.
*   **Ira Corp:** The primary antagonist, specializing in "Acquisitions" and military contracting. Led by Director Vance (a brain in a jar).
*   **Solarin Industries:** Mining and heavy infrastructure.
*   **Haxaway Energy:** Controls the fuel supply.
*   **Omni-Medical:** Biotech and combat drugs.
*   **Stance:** They view the Outer System not as a nation, but as a market to be exploited and a source of "Human Assets."

### 4. The Void-Runners (The Syndicate)
A decentralized criminal syndicate operating on **The Feed**, an uncrackable dark-net platform. They are **not** a political power, but a cultural and economic force.
*   **Nature:** A symbiosis of "Streamers" (Influencers/Smugglers who monetize crime) and "Scrappers" (Industrial mechanics).
*   **The Secret (DEV ONLY):** The Feed is actually run by **ZERO**, a surviving True AI from the ancient purges. Zero uses the runners to destabilize the system, ensuring no human faction becomes strong enough to hunt it down.
*   **Role:** They act as mercenaries, smugglers, and chaos agents, turning blockade running into entertainment for the bored masses of the Core Worlds.

## The Jovian Sisters ("The Family")
They are **not** a government; they are a family of genetically engineered post-humans.
*   **Nature:** The children of the scientist **Jupiter (The Mother)**. Each is unique, representing a different facet of transhuman evolution (Psychic, Cybernetic, Biological, etc.).
*   **Relationship:** They live among the Confederacy. Some fight for it, some hide within it, and some just want to be left alone.
*   **Visuals:** Clean, medical, "Bio-punk," almost alien in their perfection. Bioluminescent accents and organic curves.

## Systemic Threats

### The Voidborn (Cosmic Horror)
*   **Nature:** Enigmatic entities from the space between stars. They are "That-between-gravity."
*   **Manifestation:** They corrupt reality, silence stars, and are heard as "Whispers" by powerful psychics like **Europa** and **Metis**. They are the existential threat that Jupiter feared.

## Key Story Arcs

### The Europa Incident
A rogue bioweapon threat on Europa uncovered by **Elara** and **Himalia**. It ended when **Europa** (the sister) unleashed her full psychic might, decimating a corporate compound. The city of **Azure** used this event as a diplomatic lever, refusing to hand Europa over and sparking the alliance that became the Confederacy.

### The Shadow War
Ira Corp has moved from corporate espionage to direct hostile action, abducting sisters like **Thebe**, **Pasiphae**, and **Metis** to black sites on **Titan**. This escalation continues as a shadow war: The Republic forms committees, the Void-Runners livestream the chaos, and in the dark, the Voidborn are watching.

## Technology and Robotics

### General Tech Level
Humanity has mastered the solar system but is bound by the speed of light.
*   **Propulsion:** High-efficiency fusion drives allow for travel between planets in weeks.
*   **Energy:** Fusion is abundant.

### Robotics & AI
*   **Drones:** Ubiquitous.
*   **Mechs:** Used for heavy labor and heavy combat.
*   **Artificial Intelligence:** True AGI is rare and heavily regulated (due to ancient purges). Most "AI" are advanced expert systems. **ZERO** is a terrifying exception.

### Cybernetics vs. Bio-hacking
*   **Bio-hacking:** The Jovian specialty. Gene-editing to withstand radiation or breathe toxic air. Considered "elegant" by the Outer System.
*   **Cybernetics:** The Inner System/Corporate preference. Replacing limbs with steel, ocular implants, neural jacks. Considered "crude" but effective.
