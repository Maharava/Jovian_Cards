## Synergy Concepts

### Megacorp: "Profit at any cost"
1.  **Liquidate**
    *   **Condition:** Active if a **friendly unit died** (or was sacrificed) this turn.
    *   **Lore:** *The "Downsizing" philosophy. Megacorp equipment is insured; when it breaks, the company often makes a profit. This rewards the "suicide swarm" playstyle.*
    *   **Example:** "OnPlay: Deal 2 damage. **Liquidate:** Deal 4 damage instead."

2.  **Quota X**
    *   **Condition:** Active if you control **X or more units**.
    *   **Lore:** *Industrial scale. Middle-managers and Executives only grant bonuses when the production floor (Board) is running at full capacity.*
    *   **Example:** "**Quota 4:** This unit gains +2/+2."

3.  **Leverage**
    *   **Condition:** Active if the **target (or enemy commander)** has a negative status effect (Hack, Weak, Stun).
    *   **Lore:** *Exploitation. Megacorp thrives when the opponent is legally or physically crippled. It turns debuffs into execution triggers.*
    *   **Example:** "**Leverage:** Destroy target unit."

### Confederate: "Stronger together"
1.  **Unity**
    *   **Condition:** Active if you control **another Confederate unit**.
    *   **Lore:** *The "Family" mechanic. The Jovian Sisters and the Militia are defined by their bonds. Isolated, they are vulnerable; together, they cover each other's weaknesses.*
    *   **Example:** "**Unity:** Gain **Shield**."

2.  **Flank**
    *   **Condition:** Active if **another friendly unit attacked** this turn.
    *   **Lore:** *Guerrilla warfare. The Confederates rely on distraction and combined arms. One unit suppresses the enemy so the second can land the killing blow.*
    *   **Example:** "**Flank:** This unit gains **Double Attack** this turn."

3.  **Spark**
    *   **Condition:** Active if you have played a **Tactic card** this turn.
    *   **Lore:** *Tech-weaving. The Confederates are scrappy; they weave makeshift gadgets, hacking tools, and medical supplies into their unit maneuvers.*
    *   **Example:** "**Spark:** This unit costs 1 less."

### Republic: "Order and Logistics"
1.  **Protocol**
    *   **Condition:** Active if this is the **first card you played** this turn.
    *   **Lore:** *Strict adherence to procedure. The Republic war machine functions best when following the pre-approved battle plan without deviation.*
    *   **Example:** "**Protocol:** Gain +1/+1."

2.  **Majority**
    *   **Condition:** Active if you have **more cards in hand** than your opponent.
    *   **Lore:** *Resource superiority. The Republic drowns enemies in logistics. If they have more options (cards) than you, they are winning.*
    *   **Example:** "**Majority:** Draw 1 card."

3.  **Entrench**
    *   **Condition:** Active if you control a unit with **Guard**.
    *   **Lore:** *Holding the line. The Republic infantry anchors the line, allowing specialized units to operate safely behind the wall of plasteel.*
    *   **Example:** "**Entrench:** Deal 2 damage to random enemy at end of turn."
