# Jovian Cards: Design Scratchpad
*This file tracks brainstorming ideas, concepts, and "maybe" features. Nothing here is final until moved to official docs.*

## Core Lore Concepts (Brainstorming)

### Voidborn ("The Silencers")
*   **Concept:** Mathematical erasure, not biological consumption.
*   **Visuals:** Hard geometry, prisms, glitches, absence of light/sound.
*   **Goal:** "Fix the glitch" (Humanity/Psychics).
*   **Contrast:** Anti-chaos, anti-noise.

### The Bloom ("The Fever")
*   **Concept:** Life overclocked, aggressive vitality, not rot.
*   **Visuals:** Neon pinks/greens, heat, boiling blood, infinite growth.
*   **Goal:** Burn out the infection (Voidborn/Republic) by raising the "temperature".
*   **Contrast:** Hyper-chaos, hyper-noise.
